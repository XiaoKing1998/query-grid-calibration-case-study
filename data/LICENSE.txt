SCOPE

The tables in data/manifests and data/derived are a research subset and
transformation of the existing Real-IAD case-study records. Dataset-relative
image identifiers, acquisition-group identifiers, camera identifiers and
official anomaly annotations refer to Real-IAD and retain the applicable
upstream CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 terms. They are not original software licensed under
MIT or Apache-2.0.

The study adds fitting/calibration/development role assignments, candidate
and prototype identities, scalar predictions, regional score summaries,
matching diagnostics and aggregate statistical results. These are identified
as study-generated additions to this derived research-data package. To the
extent copyright or database rights apply to the adapted tables supplied
here, this package is distributed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. This notice does not
claim new ownership over individual numerical facts or expand the upstream
licensor's rights.

The data tables are not the original complete dataset or its original label
archive. No source image, mask, dense feature/map, model checkpoint or reserved
confirmation-set record is included. Obtain the source assets directly from
the official host and comply with its access conditions. Publication of this
derived subset does not waive those conditions or grant access to gated files.
